Meet the founder

Eric Sherwood

Owner & Principal Contractor of Sherwood Inc.

Eric Sherwood's construction journey began in June 1979 when he asked his neighbor for a summer job. This led to years of hands-on experience in various aspects of construction, from basic labor to framing and eventually running his own business.

"We're blessed to help clients fulfill their dreams by converting their homes and properties into what they've envisioned, while working with a great team of designers, engineers, and subcontractors focused on providing exactly what is expected."
— Eric Sherwood

Our approach

A clear path from first talk to final key.

Most of our work comes through referrals — and every project follows the same honest, transparent approach.

Step 01

A two-way interview

We start with an initial two-way interview process to ensure a mutual fit — getting to know you, your personality and what's most important to you.

Step 02

Design & thorough budgeting

We bring in professional architects and designers to fit your design concept, with thorough budgeting and allowances for unknowns.

Step 03

Build with transparency

Projects are performed on a "time and materials" basis with transparent billing — and on-site orchestration, coordination and execution.

Step 04

Completion & occupancy

We leave nothing unfinished — from design through completion to occupancy. If you would hire us again, we've done our job right.
